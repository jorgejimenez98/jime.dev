## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers and companies evaluating George Jimenez for senior frontend / full-stack roles. They arrive from LinkedIn, a CV or a search for his name, usually between other tasks, and decide in under a minute whether to keep reading. The job to be done: understand who George is and what he does, see proof (where he worked, what he built, how he writes), and download his CV or reach him on LinkedIn.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: precise, warm, calm.
- Voice: plain, first person, specific. Facts over adjectives; no hype, no buzzwords.
- Emotional goal: quiet confidence. The site should feel like a senior engineer who doesn't need to shout.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Visual system: Crealix AI's tokens (cool oklch neutrals, cyan primary used sparingly, Geist, soft radii, hairline borders, soft shadows only on floating UI).
- Reference: conordewey.com — a single centred reading column, an intro with avatar and links, then short lists ("Recent writing", "All posts →").
- Anti-references: developer portfolios with gradient or glowing heroes, card grids with icons, skill progress bars, typing effects, scroll-triggered entrance animations, emoji.
- Theme: light and dark, following the visitor's system setting.
- Motion: none on load or on scroll entrance (speed first). Only hover/focus transitions.
- Approved mockups (v1): home, websites (rows with real screenshots), blog post and about.

### Design Principles
1. The first screen answers "who and what" in large type, with the CV one click away.
2. One column, left-aligned text, generous spacing; hierarchy comes from size, weight and space, not boxes.
3. Lists are rows, not cards; proof (companies, real websites, dates) beats description.
4. The accent colour is rare: the primary action and focus states only.
5. Every page is static and light; no client JavaScript unless it earns its place.
